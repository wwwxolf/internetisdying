A SMALL FIELD GUIDE

Think first.
Cluck later.

SAVE: get the chicken into the coop. You can place a crumb on the coop.

KILL: make it eat all three crumbs, then reach the server. The coop ends the run.

It smells crumbs up to 3 walking steps away, around firewalls. Closest crumb wins; ties follow placement order. Without a crumb in range, it takes the shortest path to the server. Equal paths prefer right, down, left, up.

Tap a placed crumb to remove it. Use arrow keys to move between tiles and Enter or Space to place a crumb.

Instant retry. No timer. Every route is deterministic.

This puzzle saves progress on your device. It does not change the classic V4 world's HP or rankings.
